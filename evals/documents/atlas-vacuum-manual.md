# Atlas X200 Robot Vacuum — User Manual

## First setup

Charge the Atlas X200 for at least 6 hours before first use. Place
the charging dock against a wall with 0.5 m of clearance on each
side. Download the Atlas Home app and pair the vacuum over
Bluetooth by holding the power button for 3 seconds.

## Battery and charging

The X200 uses a 5200 mAh lithium-ion battery rated for 180 minutes
of cleaning per charge. A full recharge takes 4.5 hours. The
battery is user-replaceable; replacement part number is BAT-X200-52.

## Maintenance

Empty the dustbin after every run. Wash the HEPA filter monthly
under cold water and let it dry for 24 hours before reinstalling.
Replace the side brushes every 6 months and the main roller every
12 months.

## Error codes

| Code | Meaning | Fix |
|------|---------|-----|
| E01 | Wheel stuck | Remove debris from the wheel module |
| E02 | Dustbin missing | Reinsert the dustbin until it clicks |
| E03 | Cliff sensor blocked | Wipe the sensors with a dry cloth |
| E04 | Battery too low to start | Dock the vacuum for 30 minutes |
| E05 | Wi-Fi connection lost | Restart the router and re-pair |

## Storage

For storage longer than one month, charge the battery to 60
percent, power off the unit, and keep it below 35 °C.
