# Company Policies

## Refund policy

Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase.
After 30 days, refunds are issued as store credit only. Digital
products are refundable within 14 days if unused. Refunds are
processed back to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Shipping

Standard shipping takes 5 to 7 business days. Express shipping
takes 2 business days and costs 9.99 EUR. We ship to Europe and
North Africa. Orders above 80 EUR ship free of charge.

## Warranty

All hardware products carry a 24-month limited warranty covering
manufacturing defects. Accidental damage is not covered. Warranty
claims require the original order number.

## Payments

We accept Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, and bank transfer. Bank
transfer orders are shipped only after the payment clears, which
typically takes 2 business days.

## Support

Support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00 CET, via
email at support@example.com. The average first response time is
under 4 hours.
