# Employee Handbook

## Vacation

Full-time employees receive 25 paid vacation days per year, plus
public holidays. Unused days can be carried over until March 31 of
the following year. Vacation requests need manager approval at
least 2 weeks in advance for absences longer than 3 days.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote
arrangements require director approval. Remote employees must be
reachable during core hours, 10:00 to 15:00 local time.

## Expenses

Business expenses are reimbursed within 30 days of submission
through the Expensify portal. Meals during travel are capped at
45 EUR per day. Receipts are mandatory for any expense above 10 EUR.

## Equipment

Each new employee receives a laptop, a monitor, and a 300 EUR
one-time budget for home-office equipment. Equipment remains
company property and must be returned when employment ends.

## Onboarding

New hires complete onboarding in their first 2 weeks: security
training on day 1, a buddy assignment for the first month, and a
30-60-90 day plan agreed with their manager in week 2.
